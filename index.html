02 · How boarding works

Space Disco is a party you play your way into.

  1. T-4

    Send the signal

    It started in 2019 with a text: send the word disco, get an invitation back. Today you request a pass right here.

  2. T-3

    Get your boarding pass

    Every guest gets a pass with a QR code and a flight number. You show it at the gate, and it gets scanned on the way in.

  3. T-2

    Join the crew

    Show up, dance, bring good people. Each flight moves you up the ranks, from cadet to crew to premier class.

  4. T-1

    Find the secret door

    The location arrives close to launch. Sometimes it's a back door with a code. Sometimes it's a dome in a field.

03 · Flight log

Seven years of liftoff.

  1. 2019

    Text disco

    The first Space Disco bot answers a text with an invitation and a QR code. Crew members are born.

  2. 2020

    The saucer lands

    The mirror-ball saucer and its two astronauts become the logo. The dome night fills up with light, and boarding passes start arriving by picture message.

  3. 2021

    Secret door nights

    Club nights in downtown Portsmouth, New Hampshire. Multiple DJs, a back-door entrance and a door code sent the day of. "Get ready to laugh and dance."

  4. 2022

    Mirror balls in the browser

    spacedisco.com becomes a scrolling 3D field of disco balls, and pre-boarding opens for flight SD-0004.

  5. 2023

    The music library

    A studio, a command line and a track scanner, so every set has a record of what moved the floor.

  6. 2026

    NoCap on the Seacoast

    A house music night in Stratham, New Hampshire. Invite only, vetted list, 87 tickets, a QR at the door.

  7. Now

    Countdown to the next flight

    Space Disco is back. New site, new passes, the same promise.
